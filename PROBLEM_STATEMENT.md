# F-06. Investment Portfolio Risk and Scenario Analysis
**Tags:** INVESTMENTS | RISK ANALYSIS

## Context
Investors must often make decisions across multiple assets with different levels of risk, expected returns, liquidity, and time horizons. A portfolio that appears diversified may still be exposed to common market risks, while a change in one investment can affect the overall portfolio in unexpected ways. Understanding these interactions is difficult when decisions are based on isolated assets or historical returns alone.

## Problem Statement
Develop a solution that helps investors understand the composition, performance, and risk exposures of an investment portfolio and evaluate how different market conditions could affect its value.

### Core Requirements:
1. **Portfolio & Risk Factors:** Account for asset allocation, historical performance, volatility, concentration, liquidity requirements, investment horizons, and relationships between assets.
2. **Scenario Simulation:** Examine how portfolio behaviour changes under scenarios such as:
   - Market declines
   - Interest-rate changes
   - Sector-specific disruptions
   - Fluctuations in currency values
3. **Decision Support & Guardrails:**
   - Compare alternative portfolio allocations or investment scenarios.
   - Explain the assumptions behind the analysis.
   - Identify potential conflicts between financial objectives, risk tolerance, and liquidity needs.
   - Distinguish historical observations from hypothetical outcomes and avoid presenting uncertain projections as guaranteed results.

## Expected Outcome
An interactive portfolio analysis solution that presents portfolio composition and relevant risk exposures, compares hypothetical market scenarios, and helps users understand the potential consequences and trade-offs of alternative investment decisions.
